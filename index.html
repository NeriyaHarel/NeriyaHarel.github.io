A minute a day, not a weekend of cleaning

The app looks through your photos and files in the background and sorts what it finds: bursts, look-alikes, exact copies, screenshots, old downloads and big videos. You get a short stage of a few cards, one question each.

Keep

It stays, and the app leaves it alone for months.

Later

Not sure? Ask me another day.

Delete

It goes to the app's Trash, not into thin air.

Safe first

  • Everything you remove stays in the Trash for 30 days, so you can bring it back.
  • The app suggests, you approve. For bursts and look-alikes it picks the best shot for you: sharp, eyes open, smiling.
  • Your photos are never uploaded. Everything is worked out on your phone.

Calm, every day

A gentle check at the times you choose, only when there is something to ask. A clear picture of where your space goes, by kind. Photos of people grouped on your phone into albums you can rename, merge or hide.
